1. What is This Platform?

The Toast Underwriting Platform is where you (the sales team) submit merchant applications for approval. When you sign up a new merchant who wants to process card payments, you enter their information here.

The platform does three things:

  • Collects merchant information - You fill out a form with the merchant's business details
  • Calculates risk exposure - The system automatically calculates how much financial risk this merchant represents
  • Routes for approval - Based on the risk level, the case either gets auto-approved or goes to the Risk team for review

1
Click "Submit Request" in the left sidebar
2
Fill out all the required fields (marked with *)
3
Watch the Exposure Calculator on the right side - it updates as you enter data

Review the approval tier shown (Auto-Approved, Abbreviated Review, or Full Credit Review)

Add any notes in the "Initial Notes" section
6

6. How Exposure is Calculated (The Exact Formula)

This is the exact formula the system uses:
Step 1: Calculate Daily Volume Daily Volume = Annual Processing Volume ÷ 365 Step 2: Calculate Base Exposure Base Exposure = Daily Volume × Advance Delivery Days (ADD) Step 3: Calculate Chargeback Exposure Chargeback Exposure = Daily Volume × 5% Step 4: Calculate Refund/Return Exposure Refund/Return Exposure = Daily Volume × 1% Step 5: Calculate Total Exposure TOTAL EXPOSURE = Base Exposure + Chargeback Exposure + Refund/Return Exposure

Example Calculation

Merchant Info:

  • Annual Processing Volume: $10,000,000 (ten million dollars)
  • Advance Delivery Days: 7 days

Calculation:

  1. Daily Volume = $10,000,000 ÷ 365 = $27,397.26
  2. Base Exposure = $27,397.26 × 7 = $191,780.82
  3. Chargeback Exposure = $27,397.26 × 0.05 = $1,369.86
  4. Refund/Return Exposure = $27,397.26 × 0.01 = $273.97
  5. TOTAL EXPOSURE = $191,780.82 + $1,369.86 + $273.97 = $193,424.65

Result: This case has an exposure of about $193K, which is under $200K, so if ADD is 3 days or less, it would be auto-approved.

Another Example

Merchant Info:

  • Annual Processing Volume: $50,000,000 (fifty million dollars)
  • Advance Delivery Days: 30 days

Calculation:

  1. Daily Volume = $50,000,000 ÷ 365 = $136,986.30
  2. Base Exposure = $136,986.30 × 30 = $4,109,589.00
  3. Chargeback Exposure = $136,986.30 × 0.05 = $6,849.32
  4. Refund/Return Exposure = $136,986.30 × 0.01 = $1,369.86
  5. TOTAL EXPOSURE = $4,109,589 + $6,849.32 + $1,369.86 = $4,117,808.18

Result: This case has an exposure of about $4.1 million, which is way above $500K, so it requires Full Credit Review.

Key Insight: The main driver of exposure is Annual Processing Volume × Advance Delivery Days. Higher volume or more days = higher exposure.

7. The Three Approval Tiers

Based on the calculated exposure AND the Advance Delivery Days, the case falls into one of three approval tiers:

Tier 1: Auto-Approved (Green)

Criteria:
  • Total Exposure is $200,000 or less, AND
  • Advance Delivery Days is 3 days or less
What Happens: Case is approved instantly. No human review needed.

Tier 2: Abbreviated Review (Blue)

Criteria:
  • Total Exposure is more than $200,000 but less than $500,000, AND
  • Advance Delivery Days is between 4 and 45 days
What Happens: Case goes to the Risk team for a quick review. They check the basic information and approve or decline.

Tier 3: Full Credit Review (Red)

Criteria:
  • Total Exposure is $500,000 or more, AND
  • Advance Delivery Days is more than 45 days
What Happens: Case requires detailed Risk team analysis. Additional documentation may be required.
Manual Review: If a case doesn't fit neatly into the above categories (e.g., high exposure but low ADD, or low exposure but high ADD), it goes to Manual Review where the Risk team decides how to handle it.

8. After You Submit - What Happens Next

If Auto-Approved

  • You'll see a success message immediately
  • The case status shows "Auto Approved"
  • No further action needed from you

If Sent for Risk Review

  • The case status shows "Pending Approval"
  • The Risk team will review your submission
  • They may:
    • Approve - Case is approved
    • Decline - Case is rejected (you'll see the reason)
    • Request Revision - They need more information from you

If Revision is Requested

  • You'll see the case status change to "Revision Requested"
  • Check the case details to see what information is needed
  • Edit the case with the requested changes
  • Resubmit for review

10. Common Questions

Q: Can I edit a case after submitting?

Only if the Risk team requests a revision. Otherwise, submitted cases cannot be edited.

Q: How long does approval take?

  • Auto-approved: Instant
  • Abbreviated Review: Usually 1-2 business days
  • Full Credit Review: 3-5 business days or more

Q: What if I made a mistake on the form?

Contact your administrator or the Risk team. They can request a revision which will allow you to edit the case.

Q: Why was my case declined?

Click on the case to see the decline reason. Common reasons include:

  • Risk exposure too high for the merchant's profile
  • Insufficient documentation
  • High-risk industry without proper safeguards
  • Previous issues with the merchant

Q: Can I see cases submitted by other sales team members?

By default, you only see your own cases. Administrators can see all cases.

Q: How do I export my cases?

On the dashboard, look for the "Export" button. You can export to CSV (for Excel) or PDF.
