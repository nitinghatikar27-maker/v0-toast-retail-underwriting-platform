1. Platform Overview

What is Toast?

Toast is a web-based underwriting system designed for retail payment processing companies. It helps manage the approval process for new merchant accounts by:

  • Collecting merchant account information from customers
  • Automatically calculating risk exposure based on business metrics
  • Routing cases through appropriate approval workflows
  • Tracking all approvals and decisions in an audit trail
  • Providing dashboards for monitoring and reporting

Core Purpose

When a company wants to accept card payments through your platform, they submit information. Toast analyzes their business to determine:

  • How much money they process annually
  • How risky their business is
  • What approval process they need to go through
  • Who needs to approve their account

2. How It Works - Simple Overview

The Basic Flow

1 Merchant Submits Info: Company fills out a form with their details (name, business type, annual processing volume, etc.)
2 System Calculates Risk: Toast calculates how much money they handle and how long they can wait for funding
3 Automatic Decision or Review: Based on the calculation:
  • If LOW RISK → Auto-approved immediately
  • If MEDIUM RISK → Sent to Risk team for review
  • If HIGH RISK → Sent to Risk team with special documentation
4 Risk Team Approves/Declines: Risk approvers review the case and make final decision
5 Merchant Gets Answer: Company is notified of approval or decline

3. User Types & Permissions

Three Types of Users

Account Executives (Users)

What They Do: Submit new merchant applications

Permissions:

  • Can submit new cases
  • Can edit their own draft cases (before submission)
  • Can view all their submitted cases
  • Can add notes/comments to their cases
  • CANNOT approve cases
  • CANNOT see other team members' cases (unless admin)

Risk Approvers

What They Do: Review and approve/decline submitted cases

Permissions:

  • Can view all submitted cases (global view)
  • Can approve cases (with optional comments)
  • Can decline cases (with reason required)
  • Can request revisions from submitters
  • Can add notes to case activity
  • Can view full audit trail
  • Each approver has an approval limit (max $ they can approve)
Example: If an approver's limit is $500K and a case needs $600K approval, it goes to a higher-level approver.

Admins

What They Do: Manage users, view reports, configure system

Permissions:

  • Can do everything Users and Approvers can do
  • Can create, edit, delete user accounts
  • Can set user roles and approval limits
  • Can view system-wide reports and analytics
  • Can export data to CSV/PDF
  • Can view all cases regardless of who submitted

5. Exposure Calculation - How Risk is Measured

What is Exposure?

Exposure is a dollar amount that represents the company's maximum monthly risk to the platform.

The Exact Calculation Formula

This is the precise formula used by the system to calculate total exposure:

Step 1: Calculate Daily Volume Daily Volume = Annual Processing Volume / 365 Step 2: Calculate Base Exposure Base Exposure = Daily Volume × Advance Delivery Days (ADD) Step 3: Calculate Chargeback Exposure Chargeback Exposure = Daily Volume × 5% Step 4: Calculate Refund/Return Exposure Refund/Return Exposure = Daily Volume × 1% Step 5: Calculate Total Exposure TOTAL EXPOSURE = Base Exposure + Chargeback Exposure + Refund/Return Exposure

Worked Example

Company Example:
  • Annual Processing Volume (APV): $10,000,000
  • Advance Delivery Days (ADD): 5 days

Calculation:

  • Step 1: Daily Volume = $10,000,000 / 365 = $27,397.26 per day
  • Step 2: Base Exposure = $27,397.26 × 5 = $136,986.30
  • Step 3: Chargeback Exposure = $27,397.26 × 5% = $1,369.86
  • Step 4: Refund Exposure = $27,397.26 × 1% = $273.97
  • Step 5: TOTAL EXPOSURE = $136,986.30 + $1,369.86 + $273.97 = $138,630.13

This exposure of $138,630 would trigger: Abbreviated Review Tier (between $200K-$500K is abbreviated, but this is under $200K so it's AUTO-APPROVED)

Another Example (High Risk)

Company B:
  • Annual Processing Volume (APV): $50,000,000
  • Advance Delivery Days (ADD): 50 days

Calculation:

  • Step 1: Daily Volume = $50,000,000 / 365 = $136,986.30 per day
  • Step 2: Base Exposure = $136,986.30 × 50 = $6,849,315
  • Step 3: Chargeback Exposure = $136,986.30 × 5% = $6,849.32
  • Step 4: Refund Exposure = $136,986.30 × 1% = $1,369.86
  • Step 5: TOTAL EXPOSURE = $6,849,315 + $6,849.32 + $1,369.86 = $6,857,534.18

This exposure of $6.8M would trigger: Full Credit Review Tier (≥$500K AND ADD >45 days)

Key Points About the Formula

  • Daily Volume is the base: Everything starts from how much they process per day
  • ADD multiplies base exposure: More days they wait for money = more risk
  • Chargeback & Refund are fixed percentages: Always 5% and 1% of daily volume
  • Total Exposure determines approval tier: Not just APV alone, but the combined exposure
  • Used with ADD for tier decision: Tier is based on both exposure amount AND advance delivery days

6. Approval & Risk Process - How Cases Get Approved

Three Approval Tiers (Based on Exposure + Days)

Tier 1: Auto-Approved

Criteria:

  • Exposure ≤ $200,000 AND
  • Advance Delivery Days ≤ 3

Process: Automatically approved on submission, no human review needed

Status: Goes straight to "Approved"

When: Usually for established retailers with low risk

Tier 2: Abbreviated Review

Criteria:

  • Exposure > $200,000 AND < $500,000 AND
  • Advance Delivery Days between 4-45

What Happens:

  1. Sales team submits the request form (same as auto-approved cases)
  2. System calculates exposure and sees it exceeds $200K threshold
  3. Case status becomes "Pending Risk Approval"
  4. Risk team gets notified there's a case to review
  5. Risk team reviews the basic information submitted
  6. Risk approver either Approves, Declines, or Requests Revisions

Status Path: Submitted → Pending Risk Approval → Approved/Declined/Revision Requested

Note: For Abbreviated Review, the sales team does NOT need to fill out any additional forms. The Risk team reviews what was already submitted in the original request form.

Tier 3: Full Credit Review

Criteria:

  • Exposure ≥ $500,000 AND
  • Advance Delivery Days > 45

What Happens:

  1. Sales team submits the request form
  2. System calculates exposure and sees it's a high-risk case
  3. Case status becomes "Pending Risk Approval"
  4. Sales team must complete the Manual Review Form with additional details
  5. Risk team performs detailed analysis
  6. Senior risk approver makes final decision

Status Path: Submitted → Pending Risk Approval (complete manual form) → Risk Review → Approved/Declined

THE MANUAL REVIEW FORM (For Full Credit Review Cases)

When a case requires Full Credit Review, the sales team must provide additional information by completing the Manual Review Form. Here's exactly what needs to be filled out:

Section A - Merchant Information (Pre-filled, can be edited)

This section is already filled from the original request. Sales can update if needed:

Field What to Enter
Parent Company Name Legal name of the parent company

DBA Doing Business As name
MCC Merchant Category Code (e.g., 5812)

Brick & Mortar Yes or No - Does merchant have physical locations?

Processing details from original submission, plus new fields:

Field What to Enter

Average Ticket Size Average transaction amount in USD
CNP Volume % Percentage of Card Not Present transactions
Advance Delivery Days Days between payment and delivery
Refund/Return Rate % NEW for manual form - Historical refund rate percentage
Chargeback Rate % NEW for manual form - Historical chargeback rate percentage

Section C - Exposure Calculator (Auto-calculated)

The system automatically calculates and displays:

  • Daily Volume
  • Base Exposure
  • Chargeback Exposure (5%)
  • Refund/Return Exposure (1%)
  • Total Exposure

Sales team does NOT need to calculate this - the system does it automatically based on the processing profile entered.

Section D - Reserves & Guarantees

This is where sales documents any risk mitigation measures:

Reserve Options:

Option Description What to Enter
Reserve Percentage Percentage of transactions held as reserve Enable toggle, enter percentage (0-100)
Capped Reserve Fixed dollar amount held as reserve Enable toggle, enter dollar amount

Guarantee Options:

Guarantee Type What It Is What to Enter
Personal Guarantee Business owner personally guarantees payment Enable toggle, enter guarantor's name
Corporate Guarantee Parent company guarantees payment Enable toggle, enter guarantor company name

Section E - Case Description

A detailed narrative about the case:

Field What to Enter
Case Description Detailed explanation of why this merchant should be approved despite high exposure. Include business history, growth plans, industry context, and any mitigating factors.
Important: This is the sales team's opportunity to "sell" the case to the Risk team. Be thorough and include all relevant information that supports approval.

Section F - Snapshot (Business Proof)

Upload visual proof of the business:

  • Click "Upload Snapshot Image" button
  • Upload a screenshot or photo showing the business is legitimate
  • Examples: Website screenshot, storefront photo, product images
  • Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, GIF

Saving and Submitting the Manual Form

Save Draft: Click "Save Draft" to save progress without submitting. You can return later to complete.

Submit for Approval: When all sections are complete, click "Submit for Approval" to send to Risk team.

! The case will change from "Pending Risk Approval" to active review status once submitted.

What Happens During Risk Approval

1 Risk Team Reviews: Looks at company info, exposure calculation, and all supporting docs
2 Risk Team Can:
  • Approve: Case is approved, can proceed
  • Decline: Case is rejected with explanation
  • Request Revisions: Ask submitter for more info and resubmit
  • Request Higher Approval: If case exceeds approver's limit
3 Decision is Documented: All decisions logged in audit trail with comments

Approval Limits

Each approver has a maximum exposure they can approve. Examples:

  • Junior Approver: Can approve up to $300,000 exposure
  • Senior Approver: Can approve up to $1,000,000 exposure
  • VP/Director: Can approve unlimited amounts

If a case exposure exceeds an approver's limit, it must go to someone with higher authority.

8. Data Management

Where Data is Stored

All platform data is stored in the browser's localStorage, which means:

  • Persistent: Data stays even if browser is closed
  • Local: Data exists on the user's computer
  • Automatic: All changes save automatically
  • Size Limit: Usually 5-10MB per domain
Important: This is a local system. For production use with sensitive data, you would want to integrate with a backend database (like PostgreSQL, MongoDB, etc.).

Data That Gets Saved

  • All user accounts and roles
  • All submitted cases and their details
  • All approvals and decisions
  • All chat/comments on cases
  • Audit trail of all actions

Export Options

Admins can export data as:

  • CSV: Comma-separated values (open in Excel)
  • PDF: Professional formatted report

Can export:

  • All cases
  • Approval decisions and timeline
  • User activity reports
  • Audit trail

9. Audit & Compliance

What Gets Tracked

Every action in the system is logged, including:

Action Information Captured
Case Submitted Who submitted, when, all data provided
Case Approved Who approved, when, approval limit used, comments
Case Declined Who declined, when, reason provided
Case Revised Who revised, what changed, when
Comments Added Who commented, what they said, when
User Actions Login, logout, role changes

Audit Trail Example

Case #1001 - ABC Retail Corp Timeline: - 2024-04-09 10:15 - John Smith submitted case with $250K exposure - 2024-04-09 10:20 - System calculated: Abbreviated Review needed - 2024-04-09 11:30 - Sarah Johnson reviewed case - 2024-04-09 11:45 - Sarah requested revisions: "Need more info on CNP volume" - 2024-04-09 14:00 - John Smith resubmitted with updated CNP data - 2024-04-09 14:30 - Sarah Johnson approved case

Compliance Features

  • No Deletions: Cases cannot be deleted, only archived
  • Full History: All changes are recorded with timestamps
  • User Attribution: Every action shows who did it
  • Immutable Records: Once logged, audit entries cannot be changed
  • Export for Audit: Full audit trail can be exported for compliance review

Additional Technical Details

Case Number Format

Each case gets a unique case number:

  • Format: "CASE-" followed by auto-incrementing number
  • Example: CASE-1001, CASE-1002, etc.
  • Purpose: Easy reference for all communications

Status Workflow

Draft ↓ [Submit] ↓ Auto Approved → APPROVED (Case Complete) OR Pending Risk Approval ↓ [Risk Approver Reviews] ↓ Approved → APPROVED (Case Complete) OR Declined → DECLINED (Case Complete) OR Revisions Requested → Back to Draft

User Session

  • Users login with email and password
  • Session stays active until they logout
  • Each user sees data based on their role
  • Admins see everything, others see only relevant cases

Performance Notes

  • System caches frequently accessed data
  • Exposure calculations are done in real-time
  • All operations are fast (no backend calls)
  • Works offline after initial load

Summary: How Tech Team Should Think About It

The platform is essentially a state machine where:

  • Input: Merchant submits business information
  • Processing: System calculates exposure and determines approval tier
  • Routing: Cases route to Auto-Approve, Abbreviated, or Full Review path
  • Approval: Risk team approves/declines based on exposure and limits
  • Output: Final decision is made and logged
  • Audit: Complete history available for compliance

The whole system ensures that no case falls through the cracks and every decision is traceable and reversible.
